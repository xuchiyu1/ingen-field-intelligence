Label from the Taxonomy Guide only. One primary category: where the robot failed, not the trigger.
Severity

Rules

Severity 1 noted, mission continued · 2 interrupted, resumed on its own · 3 human intervention needed · 4 out of service / safety risk. Rate the impact the text reports; if not described, the lowest level it supports.

Root cause stated only if the report itself says why. A guess does not count.

Keys: category letters shown on buttons · 1–4 severity · y/u root cause yes/no · k borderline · ← → move.
